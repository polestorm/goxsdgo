# Contested cases of the W3C test suite

Generated by `tools/bench` (`go -C tools/bench run . -root ../.. -docs contested`, after `w3c`);
do not edit by hand. As of 2026-11-26, W3C suite at commit `7bc3365c`, XSD 1.0 2e scope as in `TestW3CSuite`.

The W3C XML Schema test suite is the common yardstick for XSD validators, but some of its cases do not
have one right answer: the working group queried or disputed them, their outcome depends on which edition
of XML or Unicode a validator follows, or the suite expects opposite verdicts for the same situation.
Counting such cases rewards whoever happens to agree with one side, so the comparison in the README
shows the score with and without them.

The criteria were fixed in advance, before looking at who passes which case, and are the same for every
candidate. They are objective: each one is a property of the suite (its metadata, its expectations) or of
the standards it rests on, not of any validator's result. A case gets the category of the first
criterion it meets; the other criteria it also meets are listed. In the contested cases goxsdgo follows
the text of the recommendation; the cases it fails for that reason are explained one by one in
[differences.md](differences.md) (in Polish). Cases that were considered and left out are listed below with the reason.
